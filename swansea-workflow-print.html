Swansea Office Workflow

Core Highway — Job Lifecycle

Phase 1 — Job Creation
Wayne Zoe 1. Raise Job on Re-flow Book job into the diary for initial capture
Wayne Zoe 2. Arrange CAD Plans & Applications Draft up and submit any required CAD plans and/or applications
3. CAD Plan Status?
⏳ Pending
Mark "Pending" in the CAD Plan column in the diary
✅ Approved
Input the CAD link into the diary
Phase 2 — Resourcing
Tobias 4. Assign Resources Add operative name(s) and vehicle(s) to jobs in the diary.
Once fully and efficiently resourced, allocate to the operative(s) on Re-flow.
Highlight the names cell GREEN to confirm resourced.
Tobias 5. Text Job to Operative(s) Once the job has been texted over to the operative(s),
highlight the time cell BLUE in the diary to confirm.
Tobias Gareth 6. Check Plant & Equipment Speak to yard staff — do we have enough traffic signals, signs, sequential lamps, etc.?
Phase 3 — On the Road
Gareth 7. Check Paperwork Verify complete compliance once job is on the road
8. Job Status?
❌ Cancelled
Highlight entire booking text RED in the diary
✅ Active
Job continues — monitor for changes
Anyone 9. Changes While On the Road If ANYTHING changes with a job or needs to be added (manual control, maintenance, operative on site, job extended, etc.):
→ Update the diary   → Raise the event on Re-flow
Phase 4 — Costing & BoQ
10. Can You Update the BoQ?
❓ Unsure of costs
Highlight job number cell PURPLE in the diary to flag for end of month invoicing
✅ Yes
Update the BoQ with costs
Phase 5 — Invoicing & Close-out
11. Cross-Reference BoQ to Diary Double-check everything has been costed. Ensure diary entries match the BoQ.
12. Mark as Checked Highlight job number cell GREEN in the diary to confirm BoQ matches diary activity
13. Tag Job Status
🚧 Still on road
Tag as Active
✅ Completed
Tag as Ready For Invoicing

👥 Responsibilities

Wayne Zoe — Job creation, CAD plans, applications
Tobias — Resourcing (names, vehicles, allocation on Re-flow, texting operatives)
Tobias Gareth — Plant & equipment checks
Gareth — Compliance checks on the road
Anyone — Changes while job is live → diary + Re-flow

🎨 Diary Colour Key

Green names cell — Job fully resourced (names & vehicles allocated)
Blue time cell — Job texted to operative(s) ✓
Red entire booking text — Job cancelled
Purple job number cell — Costs unsure, flag for end of month invoicing
Green job number cell — BoQ checked and matches diary ✓
