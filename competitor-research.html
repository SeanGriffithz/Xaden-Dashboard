What Tradespeople Complain About

From Reddit, forums & app reviews — the top pain points

1. "I just want to send a quote and an invoice — why do I need to learn an entire operating system?"

Overwhelming complexity is the #1 complaint. Tools like ServiceM8 and Simpro have so many features that sole traders feel lost. They don't need scheduling for 20 staff — they need to quote a boiler swap in 30 seconds.

2. "I'm too busy doing actual work to mess around with apps"

Reddit thread: plumber working 8am–10pm, 7 days — "I barely have the energy to do invoices let alone new quotes." Tradespeople are time-poor. Every tap matters.

3. "I hate paying £34/month for something when I could do it on Word"

Cost sensitivity is real, especially for sole traders. Many try Word/Excel templates first, then struggle with formatting issues. They want professional output but resist monthly subscriptions.

4. "The Android app is rubbish compared to iOS"

ServiceM8 has no Android app at all. Others treat Android as second-class. Many tradespeople use Android (cheaper, more durable phones for site work).

5. "It doesn't understand UK stuff — VAT, Gas Safe, certificates"

US-origin tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro, GorillaDesk) have poor UK localisation. No MTD, no UK certificate templates, no VAT handling, pricing in USD.

6. "I can't use it without internet on site"

Patchy signal on building sites / in basements. Offline mode is essential but many apps don't support it well.

7. "Customer won't accept or pay through some random link"

Domestic customers are suspicious of payment links from unfamiliar platforms. Trust matters.

8. "No analytics — I have no idea which jobs make me money"

Tradify users specifically complained about lack of analytics. Tradespeople want to know: which job types are most profitable? Where am I losing money?

9. "Per-user pricing kills us once we hire someone"

Sole trader pays £34/month. Hires one mate = £68/month. Gets an apprentice = £102/month. Costs escalate fast.

10. "Generic invoicing tools don't get what trades need"

Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent etc. can technically invoice but don't understand quote→job→invoice workflow, materials markup, site photos, or trade certificates.

Gaps & Opportunities — Where QuotePad Can Win

🎯 1. The "Just Quotes & Invoices" Gap

No tool nails the ultra-simple, quote-focused experience for sole traders. Everything tries to be a full job management platform. QuotePad can own the "I just need to quote and invoice" segment — the plumber who wants to fire off a professional quote from the van in 20 seconds.

🎯 2. Genuinely Free Tier

Not a single major competitor offers a free tier. They all start at £13–47/month. A generous free tier (e.g., 10 quotes/month, basic invoicing) would be a massive differentiator. Land them free, convert to paid when they scale.

🎯 3. UK-First, Not UK-Adapted

Only Powered Now and Tradify are truly UK-focused. But Powered Now is complex and Tradify costs £34/mo. A UK-native app that understands VAT, MTD, Gas Safe, NAPIT, NICEIC, Part P from day one — include UK supplier integrations (Screwfix, Toolstation, Plumbase).

🎯 4. AI-Powered Quote Generation

Nobody is doing this well yet. Tradesperson speaks or types "boiler swap, Worcester Greenstar 30i, 3-bed terrace, 2 radiators" and AI generates a full itemised quote with suggested pricing. Voice-to-quote for dirty hands on site.

🎯 5. WhatsApp-Native Delivery

UK tradespeople communicate with customers via WhatsApp, not email. Yet every quoting app sends quotes by email. Send professional quotes and invoices directly via WhatsApp (with PDF attached).

🎯 6. No Per-User Pricing

Flat monthly fee regardless of team size (at least up to 5 users). This removes the #1 barrier to team adoption. Tradify at £34/user kills small teams.

🎯 7. Offline-First Mobile Experience

Build for patchy signal from day one. Full offline quote creation and sending (queued). Both iOS and Android with equal quality.

🎯 8. Instant Professional Output

Beautiful, trade-appropriate templates that look professional without effort — not generic corporate invoice templates, but ones that feel right for a plumber or sparky.

🎯 9. Quote Analytics & Win Rates

"Which quotes am I winning? What's my conversion rate? Which job types are most profitable?" Nobody does this well at the sole trader level. Simple dashboards showing quote performance.

🎯 10. Speed Above All Else

Tradify claims 47 seconds for a quote. QuotePad should target 15 seconds. Pre-loaded common jobs for each trade, smart defaults, one-tap send. The fastest quoting experience on the market.
